About Us

At Yugans Product, we believe that real health begins at home — in the kitchen, with ingredients that are pure, honest, and prepared with care. Our journey started with a simple idea: to bring back the goodness of traditional, homemade food products and make them easily available to every household at a reasonable price. Today, Yugans Product proudly offers a range of wholesome food items including Multigrains Mix, Chicken Masala, Mutton Masala, Turmeric Powder, and Idly Powder, all prepared using time-tested methods and authentic ingredients.

Our Story

Yugans Product was born out of a passion for healthy living and traditional cooking. In a world filled with highly processed foods and artificial additives, we wanted to create products that reflect the purity and taste of home-cooked preparations. What began as small-batch production for family and friends soon grew into a trusted brand, as more people appreciated the quality, aroma, and authentic flavor of our products.
Every product we sell is homemade, carefully prepared in hygienic conditions, and crafted with attention to detail — just like how you would make it for your own family.

What Makes Our Products Special

At Yugans Product, quality is never compromised. We source high-quality raw ingredients, clean them thoroughly, and process them using traditional techniques that help retain natural nutrients, flavor, and aroma.
Our products are free from artificial colors, preservatives, and harmful additives, making them a healthier choice for daily cooking.

Homemade with Care

What truly sets Yugans Product apart is our homemade approach. Each batch is prepared in small quantities to ensure consistency, freshness, and quality. We believe that food made with care not only tastes better but also nourishes the body and mind.
From selecting ingredients to grinding, blending, and packing, every step is handled with dedication and responsibility. This hands-on process allows us to maintain high standards while delivering products that customers can trust.
We strongly believe that healthy and quality food should be accessible to everyone. That’s why Yugans Product offers premium homemade products at reasonable and fair prices. By keeping our operations simple and efficient, we ensure that customers receive the best value without paying extra for unnecessary packaging or branding costs.

Our Commitment

At Yugans Product, customer satisfaction is at the heart of everything we do. We are committed to:
Delivering fresh and high-quality homemade products - Preserving traditional recipes and cooking methods - Maintaining transparency and trust with our customers - Continuously improving our products based on customer feedback.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted household name for homemade food products, known for purity, authenticity, and affordability. We aim to expand our product range while staying true to our roots — offering food that feels like it’s made at home, for home.

Thank You for Choosing Yugans Product

When you choose Yugans Product, you are not just buying food — you are supporting traditional cooking, homemade quality, and honest effort. We are grateful for your trust and look forward to being a part of your everyday meals.
